FAQ

Questions, answered

How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?+

We roast every Tuesday and Friday and ship the same or next day, so most bags arrive 2 to 5 days after roasting, right in the ideal resting window.

Whole bean or ground?+

Whole bean by default. At checkout you can choose a grind for phin, espresso, pour-over, AeroPress or French press.

Do you ship outside Vietnam?+

Not yet. We currently deliver to all 63 provinces. International shipping is planned for next year.

Do you offer subscriptions or wholesale?+

Yes. Subscriptions save 10% and can be paused any time. Cafés and offices can request wholesale pricing through the contact form below.

What if I don't like a coffee?+

Tell us within 14 days and we will send a different lot or refund you, no return needed.
